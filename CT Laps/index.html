Our approach

Built once, maintained properly.

CT Labs is the group's internal software studio. We do not move fast and break things. We design carefully, build well, and support what we ship.

01

Works on your device

We build for the hardware and networks people actually have — not the newest phone or the fastest connection.

02

Designed from use

Every feature starts with how real people work. We do not add features to look impressive; we cut until what is left is exactly what is needed.

03

Supported

We maintain what we ship. When a bug surfaces, we fix it. Our apps do not drift into neglect.

What we build

Products that do the work.

From operational tools for the Yiga wings to platforms available to outside clients, our software is built around genuine problems.

01

Internal Tools
