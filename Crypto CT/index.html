Key terms

Start with the words.

If you do not know what a term means, you cannot safely evaluate a decision that uses it. Here are the three most important definitions to start with.

Blockchain

A shared record book that no single person controls. Every entry is verified by a network of computers and cannot be changed once written. It is a technology, not a currency.

Wallet

Software (and sometimes hardware) that stores the private keys needed to prove ownership of digital assets. Losing your key means losing access. There is no password reset.

Volatility

How quickly and widely the price of an asset moves. Digital assets are highly volatile — meaning large gains and large losses both happen quickly and without warning.
