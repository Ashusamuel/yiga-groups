Why FXCT

Discipline before trades.

Most people lose money trading because they start trading before they understand the market. FXCT exists to reverse that. We teach the discipline first.

01

Education first

We do not offer signals, tips or managed accounts. We teach how markets work, how to read them, and how to manage risk.

02

Risk honest

Every lesson starts with what can go wrong. We are clear about loss probability before we discuss opportunity.

03

Skill-based

Real trading skill takes time. Our programmes are built around practice, review and iteration — not passive video watching.

How we teach

Structured learning, practised properly.

FXCT's curriculum is built around three progressive stages. You move through them at your own pace, with real feedback at each step.

01

Foundations

Market structure, currency pairs, how forex works, reading charts and understanding economic data. For complete beginners. No prior knowledge assumed.

02

Technical Analysis

Support and resistance, indicators, candlestick patterns, risk-reward ratios and trade journaling. Where discipline starts to replace guessing.

03

Live Practice

Simulated trading with a virtual account, reviewed by instructors. No real money. The goal is to build a documented track record before risking anything.
