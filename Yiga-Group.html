The eight wings

A directory of every venture under the YIGA name — what it does, and why it exists.

A digital community and empowerment movement built to help people develop real skills, useful knowledge, and access to opportunity — the entry point into everything the group stands for. Life Changers exists to make sure that as YIGA GROUPS grows, the people around it grow with it.

Wireless internet and telecommunications infrastructure built for the realities of the markets it serves — affordable, reliable and fast. CT Net Lines is the connective layer underneath the rest of the group: nothing else runs without it.

The group's software and product studio — building applications, digital platforms and technology solutions, for the other wings first and for outside clients where it makes sense. CT Labs is where every other wing's operations quietly get faster.

Acquiring land, developing property and building homes and estates across Africa. CT Builders takes the long view — real assets, built to standard, held for generations rather than flipped for a quarter.

Agricultural ventures built on sustainable, innovation-led farming practices — from land to yield. CT Farmers treats food production as infrastructure: a wing that has to work regardless of how the others are doing.

Forex education and professional trading development — built to turn curiosity about markets into discipline, and discipline into a genuine skill. FXCT trains the traders before it ever talks about the trades.

Cryptocurrency and digital-asset education, exploring the opportunities of the Web3 ecosystem responsibly. Crypto CT is one wing among eight — a way the group learns and teaches the next financial layer, not the identity of the group itself.

Promoting African music, entertainment, television, artists and culture on their own terms. CT Vibes is the wing that makes sure a business group this wide never forgets to sound, look and feel like where it came from.

The founder

Besong Yiga James

Founded YIGA GROUPS in 2017 with a simple bet: that a single group, built wing by wing across the industries Africa needed most, could move further than any single company ever could.

"You don't ask a group of this size to be one thing. You ask each wing to be excellent at its own thing, and you ask all of them to fly in the same direction."

Eight wings, one founder's conviction, and a philosophy that treats every new venture as another wing on the same body — never a replacement for the ones before it.
