Who we are

Built for communities, by communities.

Life Changers is the group's community wing. We run programmes that build real skills and connect people to real opportunities — across cities, towns and villages.

01

Skills first

We teach what people can use tomorrow — practical skills that open doors in the real economy.

02

Together

Every programme is built with community leaders, not just for them. Local knowledge shapes everything we do.

03

Long view

We measure success in years, not weeks. Real change compounds slowly — and we are patient.

What we do

Programmes that open doors.

From digital literacy to entrepreneurship, our programmes meet people where they are and take them where they want to go.

Programme 01

Digital Skills

Practical training in computers, software and the internet — for people of all ages and backgrounds. Taught in accessible language, at community centres and online.

Programme 02

Entrepreneurship

From idea to plan to first customer. We run 8-week cohorts that give aspiring business owners the tools and the network they need.

Programme 03

Mentorship

Every programme participant is matched with a working professional for ongoing guidance. We believe everyone deserves a door someone has already walked through.
