Who we are

Culture is the accent on everything.

CT Vibes exists because a business group this wide must never forget where it comes from. We make sure Yiga Groups sounds, looks and feels like Africa.

01

African-owned

We promote African artists, labels and producers. Every collaboration starts from a place of equal footing — not a charity grant or an outsider's curation.

02

Platform first

CT Vibes is a platform, not a gatekeeper. We amplify what is already great — we do not decide what African culture should be.

03

Global reach

The goal is for an artist from Bamenda or Nairobi to reach a listener in Paris or Jo'burg without losing anything that makes the music theirs.

What we do

How we amplify.

CT Vibes works across music, television, events and media to give African culture the infrastructure it deserves.

01

Artist Promotion

Distribution, placement, press and digital marketing for artists who are ready to grow. We work with the music, not against it.

02

Events & Shows

Live events, concerts and cultural shows across our cities. We produce, we promote, we host — from intimate venues to major stages.

03

Media Production

TV content, online video, podcasts and cultural programming — produced and owned by Africans, for African audiences and the diaspora.

04

Brand & Culture

Culture-led brand partnerships and creative campaigns that feel authentic because they start with the artists and communities, not a brief.
