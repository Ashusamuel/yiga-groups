How we farm

Good food starts with good practice.

CT Farmers uses sustainable methods to grow food that reaches the market in good condition. We work with local farming communities, not around them.

01

Sustainable

We use farming methods that build the soil, use water responsibly and avoid inputs that damage the land for future seasons.

02

Fresh to market

Shorter routes from farm to shelf mean fresher produce and less spoilage. We handle cold-chain and logistics ourselves where possible.

03

Community first

Our farms work with and employ local communities. Yields improve when the people doing the farming benefit from the results.

What we grow

Produce you can count on.

CT Farmers grows and distributes a range of staple and commercial crops, supplying retail, wholesale and agribusiness partners.

01

Staple Crops

Maize, cassava, plantain and root vegetables — the foundation of the regional diet and the most important crops to get consistently right, season after season.

02

Vegetables & Produce

Tomatoes, peppers, onions, leafy greens. Grown with attention to the conditions that actually matter: soil quality, water, and getting to market on time.

03

Supply Partnerships

We supply supermarkets, food processors, NGOs and catering businesses with consistent volume at agreed quality levels. SLA-backed, with traceability.
