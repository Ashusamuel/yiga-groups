Who We Are

A platform for ownership, investment, and long-term growth.

Yiga Holdings is a diversified holding and investment company dedicated to strategic asset ownership, investments, business development, and sustainable long-term expansion.

Operating with a broad sectoral outlook, we provide the robust foundation and strategic guidance necessary to scale operations, protect intellectual property, and unlock value across diverse markets. Our African roots shape our resilience, while our international outlook drives our ambition.
