Our infrastructure

Built to stay up.

CT Net Lines designs, installs and operates connectivity infrastructure across Africa. We measure ourselves by uptime, speed and response time — not promises.

01

High uptime

We target 99.5% availability. When something goes wrong, our team is already on it — before most customers notice.

02

Infrastructure grade

Our backbone runs on enterprise hardware, with redundant paths so a single failure never takes down a whole area.

03

Fast response

Technical issues are logged and triaged immediately. Residential response within 4 hours; business SLA within 2.

What we offer

Connectivity for every scale.

From a home in a tier-2 city to a campus or business park, we have a plan built for the load.

Home Broadband

Fibre and fixed-wireless home plans from 10 Mbps to 1 Gbps. Installation in 48 hours. No hidden fees — one monthly price covers everything.

Business Plans

Dedicated bandwidth, static IPs, priority SLA and 24/7 support for SMEs and enterprise. Scalable from a single office to a multi-site campus.

Infrastructure Partnerships

We work with landlords, developers and local governments to wire up new builds and underserved areas. Long-term contracts, shared infrastructure.
